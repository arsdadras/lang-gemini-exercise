# Briefs

Back to the [research office](../../../archive/offices/research/README.md). A brief is our **position on one question**,
addressed to whoever decides it: the office or the user. It is advice, never a decision. The
list is in the [catalog](../CATALOG.md#briefs).

## Rules

- **One question per brief:** `BR-<NNN>-<slug>/`, numbered in order and never reused. The
  position is in `README.md`; longer supporting analysis sits beside it in its own file.
- **The question comes first**, then the short answer, then the reasoning.
- **Every factual claim links to evidence** (`EV-...`) or a landscape page; a brief holds no
  numbers of its own.
- **Only admissible arguments:** evidence, sequencing, scope, or wrong at production scale
  ([research office rules](../../../archive/offices/research/README.md#rules)).
- **Revisable, with a trail.** When evidence changes, the brief changes and a history row says
  what moved and why. A brief that no longer applies becomes `withdrawn` or `superseded by
  BR-...` in the catalog; it isn't deleted.
- **Office-facing items** go in the brief's "For the office" section and are mirrored in the
  [outbox](../../../archive/offices/research/OUTBOX.md).
