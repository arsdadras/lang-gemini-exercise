# Risk Register

Program-level risks: what could derail the curriculum, the architecture, or the repo's
integrity. Live code defects stay in the mentor's state. Likelihood and impact: **L**ow,
**M**edium, **H**igh. Reviewed at every project boundary, or when a signal fires.

| ID | Risk | L | I | Signal | Mitigation | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| RSK-01 | **Concurrent sessions on one working tree** lose updates, collide on numbers, or leave the suite red | L | H | An unexpected diff; a red suite nobody caused; a duplicate ADR number | One role and one session at a time (ADR-0028); each app session runs in its own worktree and pushes to `main`; CI | Mitigated 2026-10-06; happened 2026-09-23 |
| RSK-02 | **Role drift:** the mentor settles architecture mid-lesson, research decides, or the lead drifts into teaching | M | M | - | - | Closed 2026-10-06: one role (ADR-0028) |
| RSK-03 | **Documentation drift during the migration:** the old mentor contract, handoff, research paths, and old roadmap disagree with the offices | H | M | A duplicated fact; a link to `docs/ROADMAP.md` or `docs/office/` | Precedence rule in `CLAUDE.md` ; LD-001, LD-002; `scripts/check_docs.py` in CI | Closed 2026-09-27: LD-001 and LD-002 verified |
| RSK-04 | **Framework churn:** patch drift hides fixed bugs, or an upgrade changes an API mid-lesson | M | M | Dependabot pull requests piling up; a changelog touching an API we teach | [Dependency policy](../library/engineering/dependency-policy.md): Dependabot, smoke runs, taught APIs at topic boundaries.
