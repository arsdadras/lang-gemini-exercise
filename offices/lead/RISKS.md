# Risk Register

Program-level risks: what could derail the curriculum, the architecture, or the repo's
integrity. Live code defects stay in the mentor's state. Likelihood and impact: **L**ow,
**M**edium, **H**igh. Reviewed at every project boundary, or when a signal fires.

| ID | Risk | L | I | Signal | Mitigation | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| RSK-01 | **Concurrent sessions on one working tree** (lead, mentor, and research, or two sessions of one role) lose updates, collide on numbers, or leave the suite red | M | H | An unexpected diff; a red suite nobody caused; a duplicate ADR number | One writer per path ([ownership.md](../../library/governance/ownership.md)); path-limited commits ([git policy](../../library/engineering/git-policy.md)); CI. The lead renumbers any colliding ADR | Open; happened 2026-09-23 |
| RSK-02 | **Role drift:** the mentor settles architecture mid-lesson, research decides, or the lead drifts into teaching | M | M | ADR-level choices in the mentor's state with no request; lead files describing lesson steps | Escalation rule; directives; the lead reviews its inbox every session | Open |
| RSK-03 | **Documentation drift during the migration:** the old mentor contract, handoff, research paths, and old roadmap disagree with the offices | H | M | A duplicated fact; a link to `docs/ROADMAP.md` or `docs/office/` | Precedence rule in `CLAUDE.md` (the lead wins); LD-001, LD-002; `scripts/check_docs.py` in CI | Open until LD-001 and LD-002 are closed |
| RSK-04 | **Framework churn:** patch drift hides fixed bugs, or an upgrade changes an API mid-lesson | M | M | Dependabot pull requests piling up; a changelog touching an API we teach | [Dependency policy](../../library/engineering/dependency-policy.md): Dependabot, smoke runs, taught APIs at topic boundaries. The pre-release is gone (`0d27fa8`) | Open |
| RSK-05 | **Single gateway:** GapGPT serves four tiers; an outage, lost credit, or model-id drift stops most runs | M | H | Auth or 404 errors across tiers | `free` tier direct to Google, `local` tier, test-mode profiles ([ADR-0015](../../docs/adr/0015-local-tier-and-test-mode-profiles.md)); ids re-verified at each radar review | Open |
| RSK-06 | **Unapproved shell in the coding coordinator** until P6-B3 | L | H | Any change widening what the coordinator's interpreter can reach | Allow-listed env, `.env` denial, banner ([ADR-0005](../../docs/adr/0005-sandbox-secret-isolation.md)); fixed in P6-B3 | Accepted until P6-B3 |
| RSK-07 | **Personal data in traces:** only P5 masks before upload; every app reports to one LangSmith project | M | M | A trace with user content from a non-P5 app | Masking in every app and one project per app (R5) | Open |
| RSK-08 | **Unverified cheap tiers:** `opensource` runs RAG grading and the Finglish repair without a measured baseline here | M | M | Worse grading or repair in eval runs | Measure with the R0 eval harness before moving more call sites ([BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md)) | Open |
| RSK-09 | **Momentum loss:** a stage too steep or too theory-heavy stalls the course (happened once, P5-B3) | M | H | Long gaps between sessions; a stage past three components per topic | Difficulty curve, one hard concept per stage, vertical slices, stop points ([PEDAGOGY.md](PEDAGOGY.md)) | Open |
| RSK-10 | **Local-only history:** commits not pushed can be lost with the machine | L | H | `main` ahead of `origin/main` | Every role pushes at session end ([git policy](../../library/engineering/git-policy.md)). The 4 local commits of 2026-09-26 are on GitHub (`handoff/local-snapshot`, `main-1le51z`) | Open until they reach `main` |
| RSK-11 | **Split work tree:** the lead works in a cloud session on a branch while the mentor and research work on local `main`; the branches diverge on shared files (`CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, ADRs) | M | M | A merge conflict; a local session reading a stale `CLAUDE.md` | The lead's branch reaches `main` by pull request first; local sessions then pull before their next commit; each role touches only its own paths | Open |
